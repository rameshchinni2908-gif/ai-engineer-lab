# [ARCHIVED] Nimbus Cloud Storage — Pricing (effective 2024-01-01, SUPERSEDED)

> **This document is kept in the corpus deliberately, as stale/contradictory material for the
> RAG failure-mode lab ("stale" failure mode).** It describes pricing that stopped being true in
> February 2026. A retrieval system with no recency awareness will happily return these numbers
> as if they were current, because they are lexically and semantically just as relevant to a
> question like "how much does the Pro plan cost?" as the current document is. A well-designed
> RAG system should either prefer the more recently dated document, surface both and flag the
> conflict, or be explicitly instructed (see the `-grounded` seed prompt version) to check for
> and call out exactly this kind of contradiction.

## Historical plans (2024-01-01 through 2026-01-31, no longer accurate)

| Plan | Price | Storage quota | Workspace members | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 / month | 10 GB total | up to 3 | community forum only |
| Pro | $9 / user / month | 250 GB pooled | up to 25 | email, 2 business day response |
| Business | $22 / user / month | 1 TB pooled | unlimited | email only |

Notably, the 2024 Pro plan was $9/user/month for 250 GB with a 25-member cap and no chat
support — all three numbers (price, quota, member cap) changed in the 2026-02-01 pricing
update, and chat support was added to Business. Any answer that cites "$9/user/month" or "250
GB" or "up to 25 members" for the *current* Pro plan is wrong and is pulling from this archived
document instead of the current one.
